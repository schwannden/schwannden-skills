# Short-form video: platform specs & caption accessibility

Reference for the format constraints a shooting-ready script should respect.
Confirm the target platform from the org's owned channels (fact sheet) before
applying — don't write to a platform the org doesn't publish on.

## Structure & retention

- **Hook → Body → Payoff (+ CTA)** is the spine. The hook opens a loop; the
  body sustains it; the payoff closes it; the CTA (optional) points somewhere.
- **Hook window ≈ first 1.5–3 seconds.** Drop-off in the first 3s is a negative
  algorithm signal on every platform — the hook has to land immediately, not
  after a slow intro.
- **Change something visually every 3–5 seconds** (new shot, angle, on-screen
  text, or motion). No single static shot should run longer than ~8 seconds.
- **Deliver on the hook's promise at the payoff** — a hook that over-promises
  and a payoff that under-delivers reads as bait and tanks completion rate.

## Per-platform length & aspect

All three are vertical **9:16**. Optimize for *completion*, so shorter usually
wins — target 15–30s unless the story genuinely needs more.

| Platform | Aspect | Practical target | Hard limit (as of writing — re-check) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 9:16 | 15–34s | up to 10 min | ~2s hooks; trending sounds matter |
| Instagram Reels | 9:16 | 15–30s | up to 3 min | ~3s polished hook; served across feed/Reels |
| YouTube Shorts | 9:16 | 15–60s | 3 min | must stay ≤3 min to be classed a Short |

Platform limits change — treat the "hard limit" column as a prompt to verify
against current docs, not a fixed fact.

## Captions & on-screen text (accessibility + silent autoplay)

Most short-form is watched muted, so on-screen text is not optional:

- **Burned-in / open captions** for all spoken dialogue — don't rely on the
  platform's auto-captions alone; they mis-transcribe names and terms.
- **Safe zones:** keep captions and key text clear of the bottom ~20% (caption
  bar, CTA buttons) and the right-edge action rail (like/share/profile icons)
  so the UI doesn't cover them.
- **Contrast & size:** legible on a phone at arm's length — high-contrast text,
  a subtle background/stroke behind captions over busy footage.
- **One idea per caption card;** sync to the audio, don't dump a paragraph.

## What to add to the script

Note the target platform, its length target, aspect (9:16), and a caption
plan (open captions + safe-zone reminder) in the script's spec header, so the
shooter knows the format constraints before filming — not after.
